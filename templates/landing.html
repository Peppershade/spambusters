Why

Built with love, not profit

"My grandma got an email saying her internet provider was cancelling her subscription and she needed to take action immediately. She was deeply worried — convinced something was really wrong. She felt hopeless. Her provider's spam filter had let it straight through."

That's how Spambusters started. Not a business idea — a phone call from someone I love, panicking over an email I could tell was fake in two seconds. But I'm not always there to check. Spam doesn't just annoy non-technical people, it genuinely frightens them. Fake cancellations, urgent account warnings, phishing links dressed up as their bank — things you and I scroll past without thinking can leave your loved ones scared, confused, or worse.

Spambusters lets you train a model on their actual inbox, learning what their spam looks like — not some generic dataset. Set it up, point it at their email, and it quietly keeps watch in the background. You get Discord alerts, they get a cleaner inbox. No installs on their end, no new apps to explain, no more panicked phone calls.

It's not for everyone. If you're on Gmail, Outlook, or Proton Mail with strong built-in filtering, you probably don't need it. Spambusters fills the gap for ISP-bundled email accounts and legacy providers where the spam filter is weak, outdated, or simply not there — the kind of inbox that's had the same address for 20 years and gets everything.

Features

Everything you need to keep them safe

Set it up once, and it works quietly in the background — learning, protecting, and alerting you when it matters.

🧠

ML-Powered Detection

A PyTorch neural network trained on their actual inbox, not a generic dataset. Combined with heuristic scoring, it learns what spam looks like for them and gets smarter over time.

🛡️

3-Stage Protection

You decide how aggressively it acts. Suspicious? Add a warning banner. Likely spam? Move it away. Obvious scam? Delete it before they ever see it.

🌍

Multi-Language Analysis

Dutch and English keyword and call-to-action detection. Because spam in your loved one's native language is exactly the kind their provider's filter ignores.

✉️

Warning Injection

Adds a visible warning banner right inside suspicious emails. They don't need a new app or any setup — the warning shows up in their normal inbox.

💬

Discord Alerts

Get notified the moment something suspicious arrives. One-click buttons let you mark it as spam, safe, whitelist or blacklist — all from your phone, wherever you are.

🔒

Secure & Mobile

Your dashboard is locked behind 2FA — only you get in. Install it as a PWA on your phone so you can check on their inbox anytime, anywhere.

Pipeline

How it works

Point it at their mailbox and it runs on its own — scanning, learning, and acting so you don't have to.

📨
Scan
Connects to their mailbox via IMAP and checks for new emails
🔬
Classify
ML model + keyword analysis scores every message
⚡
Act
Warns, moves, or deletes based on your thresholds
🔔
Notify
Pings you on Discord so you stay in the loop
📡
Start here

Learning Mode

Scans and flags suspicious emails without touching them. You review, label, and train the model on what's actually spam in their inbox. The more you teach it, the smarter it gets.

🛡️
When ready

Enforcement Mode

Once the model knows what to look for, flip the switch. Spambusters starts acting on its own — warning, moving, or deleting spam automatically. You still get Discord alerts for everything it does.
